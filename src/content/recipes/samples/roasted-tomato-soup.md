1. Roast the halved tomatoes, onion, and garlic bulb with olive oil until blistered.
2. Squeeze the garlic out of its skins into a pot with everything else.
3. Add the stock, simmer for 10 minutes, then blend with the basil.
4. Season well and loosen with water if needed.
