1. Fry the curry paste in the thick top of the coconut milk until fragrant.
2. Add the chicken and cook until coloured.
3. Pour in the rest of the coconut milk, fish sauce, and sugar and simmer.
4. Add the beans for the last 5 minutes and finish with basil.
