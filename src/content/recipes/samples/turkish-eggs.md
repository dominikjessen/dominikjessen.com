1. Stir grated garlic and a pinch of salt into the yoghurt and spread it on plates.
2. Poach the eggs and set them on the yoghurt.
3. Melt the butter with the aleppo pepper until foaming.
4. Spoon the chilli butter over, scatter dill, and serve with flatbread.
