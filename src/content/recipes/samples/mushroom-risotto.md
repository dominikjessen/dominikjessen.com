1. Brown the mushrooms hard in a little butter and set aside.
2. Soften the shallots, then toast the rice for a minute.
3. Add the wine, then the hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring.
4. Beat in the butter, parmesan, and mushrooms and rest for 2 minutes.
