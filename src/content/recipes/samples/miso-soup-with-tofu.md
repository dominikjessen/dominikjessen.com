  - name: "dried wakame"
    amount: "1 tbsp"
  - name: "spring onion"
    amount: "2"

  - vegan

timeMinutes: 10
servings: 2

1. Heat the stock until just steaming, without boiling.
2. Whisk the miso with a ladle of stock, then stir it back in.
3. Add the cubed tofu and wakame and warm through.
4. Serve topped with sliced spring onion.
