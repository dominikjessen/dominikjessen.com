1. Grill the chicken until charred and cooked through, then rest and slice.
2. Tear the sourdough and toast in olive oil until golden.
3. Blend the anchovies, yolk, lemon, parmesan, and oil into a dressing.
4. Toss the lettuce with the dressing and top with chicken and croutons.
