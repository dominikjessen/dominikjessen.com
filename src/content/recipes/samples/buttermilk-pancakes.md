1. Whisk the flour, sugar, and baking powder.
2. Beat the buttermilk, egg, and melted butter, then fold into the dry mix.
3. Rest the batter for 5 minutes.
4. Cook ladlefuls in a buttered pan until bubbles form, then flip.
5. Stack and drown in maple syrup.
