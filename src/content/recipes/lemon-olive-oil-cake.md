  - name: plain flour
    amount: 200 g
  - name: sugar
    amount: 150 g
  - name: eggs
    amount: "2"
  - name: olive oil
    amount: 120 ml
  - name: lemon zest
    amount: 2 lemons
  - name: lemon juice
    amount: 2 tbsp
  - name: baking powder
    amount: 1.5 tsp
  - name: salt
    amount: pinch
  - name: milk
    amount: 60 ml
tags:
  - vegetarian
  - make-ahead
timeMinutes: 55
servings: 8
menuLine: olive oil, lemon zest and juice, thick slices
draft: false
---

1. Whisk the wet ingredients with the zest and juice.
2. Fold in the dry ingredients and milk until just combined.
3. Bake until golden and a skewer comes out clean.
4. Cool in the tin, then slice thick.
